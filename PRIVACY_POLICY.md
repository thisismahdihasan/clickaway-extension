# ClickAway Privacy Policy

**Planned Effective Date:** November 5, 2026

**Publisher:** ClickAway

ClickAway is a browser extension that helps users perform selected repetitive workflows on supported Printify pages.

## Data ClickAway processes

ClickAway processes information that you enter or save in the extension, including product-profile names, sizes, prices, colors or variants, held variants, notes, linked product names or page paths, variation-name mappings, the selected profile, and floating-button position preferences.

While you use ClickAway on supported Printify pages, ClickAway reads relevant page content needed for the feature you selected. This can include product names, pricing-table values, variant labels and selection states, variation-name fields, and publishing-setting controls. ClickAway does not read browser cookies, passwords, payment details, or customer/order records in the current implementation.

## How data is used

ClickAway uses this data only to save your preferences and perform the Printify action that you initiate, such as applying saved prices, selecting variants, setting variation names, or applying supported publishing settings.

## Local and legacy storage

ClickAway stores its saved data in your browser's local extension storage. Older versions may have stored profiles, the selected profile, and prices in browser sync storage; ClickAway can migrate those legacy values to local storage. ClickAway does not operate an independent backend or analytics service for storing or analyzing these values.

## Sharing and transmission

The current ClickAway implementation does not send extension data to a ClickAway server, analytics provider, advertising provider, or other third party. When you start an automation action, values you choose may be entered into your existing Printify session so that Printify can process the action you requested. Printify's own terms and privacy practices apply to your use of its services.

## Backup files

When you choose to export a backup, ClickAway creates a JSON file on your device containing your saved profiles, selected profile, and legacy variation names. ClickAway reads a backup only when you select a file to import. Exported backup files remain under your control and are not automatically deleted when you delete extension data.

## Your controls and retention

You can manage individual profiles in the popup, export or restore backups from the Options page, reset floating-button positions, and use **Delete All Saved Data** in the Options page. The delete control removes ClickAway's saved local data and legacy sync data, then reloads the extension to close open views and stop active work.

Deleting extension data does not delete backup files you previously exported and does not undo actions already performed in Printify. Data remains in browser storage until you change or delete it, subject to browser behavior when an extension is uninstalled or reinstalled.

## Independent product notice

ClickAway is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Printify, Etsy, Google, or Microsoft.

## Changes to this policy

If ClickAway's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the change is released, as required by applicable law and store policy.
